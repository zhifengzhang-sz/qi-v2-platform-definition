# The market model engine: the research behind its spec

This file covers the foundation of the market model engine's objectives, requirements and features: the model its records are results of, what the engine can give at each bar and how late, how a result later revised is published on an append-only topic, and how results computed bar by bar agree with those computed on the whole series. `docs/designs/spec.md` (The market model engine; Order) states what the component must do. The requirements `records`, `refers`, `covers` and `publish` are quoted from the spec; `lean/MarketModelEngine.lean` holds `MarketModelEngine.Result`, `MarketModelEngine.Result.Keeps` and the structure `MarketModelEngine` (`worker`, `publisher`), and no `MarketModelEngine.Spec` [READ].

Sources are marked by how each was read: [READ] read in full at the part cited; [SUMMARY] from a search or fetch summary only; [INFER] an inference, not stated by a source. A lesson is cited by its number in 缠中说禅's 《教你炒股票》 (第N课), at `https://chzhshch.blog/stocks/NNN/` (NNN the number, three digits; a site that republishes the blog): the page of every lesson cited was fetched and each sentence quoted found in the lesson's own text, not its replies [READ]. A statement of the definition is marked [READ] with its file. The model of issue 1 is this file's own statement, abstracted from the lessons: its conditions are a modelling choice, not a source's statement and not a theorem; a lesson is cited where a piece of it rests on one, and each claim of it that is unproved is named so.

## 1. What model does the engine compute?

**Kind:** modelling. **Bears on:** "Give 缠论's records of its series on a topic of its own … a result series derived from the clean series" (Objectives); `records`, `refers` (Requirements); "Not required: that its results are right by 缠论's lessons … which no contract states".

**The lessons' objects.** The lessons build, in this order, "分型-笔-线段-最小级别中枢-各级别中枢、走势类型" (第72课) [READ]. Two adjacent K-lines are in 包含 when "一K线的高低点全在另一K线的范围里", and are merged into one, in order: "先用第1、2根K线的包含关系确认新的K线，然后用新的K线去和第三根比" (第62课, 第65课) [READ]. A 顶分型 is three adjacent merged K-lines of which "第二K线高点是相邻三K线高点中最高的，而低点也是相邻三K线低点中最高的", and "两个相邻的顶和底之间构成一笔" (第62课) [READ]. The 中枢: "某级别走势类型中，被至少三个连续次级别走势类型所重叠的部分，称为缠中说禅走势中枢" (第17课), its band "（max（a2,b2,c2），min（a1,b1,c1））" of the three, which must be complete: "次级别的前三个走势类型都是完成的才构成该级别的缠中说禅走势中枢" (第18课) [READ].

**The recursion.** Each level is made of the completed moves of the level below: "你决定用30分钟来操作、观察时，其实就已经先假定把所有完成的5分钟走势都看成线段了", such a 线段 being "没有内部结构的，这线段的高低点就是对应5分钟走势的高低点" (第53课) [READ]. The lessons claim the result is a function: "所谓走势必完美，就是本ID所给出的分型、笔、线段、不同级别走势类型所对应的递归函数，能将行情的任何走势唯一地分解" (第101课) [READ].

**The model.** At each level the market is a course of pieces, each in a state of {−1, 0, +1}: 0 inside a 中枢 of that level, +1 or −1 between two, by the direction in which the last was left; a bar's state at a level is its piece's. Pieces alternate 0 → ±1 → 0: a move between two 中枢 is always there, "甚至最极端的情况，可以就是一个缺口" (第29课) [READ], and a gap is a ±1 piece that holds no bar, so at the bars a 0 can follow a 0. A level is also a sequence of blocks, each a span of bars, a start and an end price, and a range. The slow level is a fixed function Φ of the fast, after 第53课 and 第101课: a 中枢 rule κ takes blocks to a course, a completion rule γ takes the course and the blocks below to the blocks above, and one Φ serves every level. The conditions on any κ: a piece is a run of blocks (K1); a 0-piece spans at least three consecutive blocks whose ranges share a point, its band the highest low and the lowest high of the first three, as 第18课's (K2); the first block of a ±1 piece ends outside the band of the 0-piece before it (K3); the course up to bar t is a function of the blocks up to some finite bar τ(t) ≥ t (K4, online). On any γ: a block is a run of the blocks below (G1), holds a whole 0-piece (G2), has its extremes at its endpoints (G3), and is online as K4 (G4). The states alone do not determine Φ, since whether three blocks overlap is a question of price: each piece carries marks, a 0-piece its band ZD to ZG and its range DD to GG, a ±1 piece its endpoints, range and duration. That the lessons' rules meet K1 to K4 and G1 to G4 is unproved.

**The random part is not the engine's.** The model gives a law to the fastest level alone, its transitions reading a hidden momentum z (力度) by assumption, and none to the slower levels, which are Φ of the fast, and of z where a rule reads it; the engine is the deterministic part, and no requirement of it reads a probability.

**What the contract's records hold of the model** [READ: `lean/MarketModelEngine.lean`, `lean/Contract/Chanlun.lean`]. `MarketModelEngine.Result` has six cases, `mergedBar`, `fractal`, `line`, `segment`, `move`, `consolidation`, each holding its record. `Chanlun.MergedBar`: `mIndex`, `up`, `start`, `end`, `confirmEnd`, `extremes`, `index`, `high`, `low`. `Chanlun.Fractal`: `mIndex` alone, with no rule (`Fractal.rules` is empty); it does not say top or bottom. `Chanlun.Line`: `start`, `end`, `confirmEnd`. `Chanlun.Segment` and `Chanlun.Move`: `start`, `end`, `confirmEnd`, `level`. `Chanlun.Consolidation`: `level`, `start`, `end`, `confirmEnd`, `zg`, `zd`, `gg`, `dd`, with `bands`: `c.dd ≤ c.zd ∧ c.zd ≤ c.zg ∧ c.zg ≤ c.gg`. So the model's 0-piece is a `Consolidation` with its marks and its span in merged bars; a block or ±1 piece is a `Line`, `Segment` or `Move`, whose direction, endpoints' prices and range are no fields: the range is read from the merged bars at its `start` and `end` only where its extremes are its endpoints (G3), which a ±1 piece need not meet and which is unproved of a 线段; a bar's state is no record; a `level` is on `Segment`, `Move` and `Consolidation` only [INFER]. The contract "says what a value is, not whether it is right by the lessons, and not how records are gathered: a run of them, their order, and which is still going on are their user's" [READ].

**The options.**
- The engine gives the pieces (the six records) and no state per bar: a reader derives a bar's state at a level from the spans of that level's `Consolidation` records. What it costs: the reader must know which result of a record counts (issue 3), and the span of a 中枢 in time is the model's rule (from the first of the three opening blocks to the block before the break), not a lesson's: the lessons read give the band, not where a 中枢 begins and ends [INFER].
- The engine gives the state of each bar at each level as results. What it costs: a seventh case of `Result` that is no record of the contract, one result per bar and level, each known late (issue 2).

**What it gives the spec.** The objective "a result series derived from the clean series" rests on the engine being a process of the K-lines (`worker`), not on the model: in the model Φ may read z, a hidden process with no measure fixed on the bars, and a library that reads a force reads a measure of its own on the K-lines it has read, so one clean series is the engine's whole input [INFER]. K1 and G1 say why naming bars is enough for `refers`: every record above the merged bar is a run of merged bars; that the bars named are among the results is the requirement itself [INFER]. "Not required: that its results are right by 缠论's lessons" rests on the lessons fixing no single function: they leave parameters, and where two of their rules meet, a turn inside a 中枢, they settle the case by a 背驰 of a lower level (issue 4), so there is no one right result series for a contract to state [INFER]. A choice left: whether a bar's state is a result.

**Gaps.** No lesson read defines the span of a 中枢 in time. That the lessons' rules meet the model's conditions is proved in no source read, and G2 fails for them in the case of a turn inside a 中枢 until that case is settled. No lesson read fixes how a 背驰 enters the rules there, and no record of the contract holds a force.

## 2. A result known only when confirmed, later than its bar: what can the engine give at each bar, and how late is a result confirmed?

**Kind:** modelling. **Bears on:** "`covers`: … one still merging, its `confirmEnd` not set, at the least, so that no k-line waits for a later one"; `refers` (a `confirmEnd` names a k-line read, or a merged bar among the results); "when it gives again a record still going on are the worker's library's" (Features).

**Confirmation.** In the model every bar has its states whether or not they are known yet; a state is known from its moment of confirmation τ_L(t), the first bar u ≥ t at which the state of bar t at level L is a function of the bars up to u, and a reading made at u ≥ τ_L(t) never changes, by that definition. The lessons say it of a figure: "一旦完成的图形，这修改就不可能了。分型可修改，证明图形没完成。" and "这图形未完成的性质就是百分百确定了，但所有图形必然完成" (第69课); "当下的分析和事后的分析，是一样的" (第65课); and name the question: "如何判断一个走势类型完成了？这是技术分析里最核心的问题之一" (第18课) [READ].

**The lag is the recursion's.** A level is a function of the completed blocks of the level below (K4, G4), so each of its records is known no earlier than they are, and a higher level can regroup a lower one after the fact: "当a+A+b时，你是不可能知道B的级别的，只是，只要b不背驰，那B至少和A同级别，但B完全有可能比A的级别大" (第32课); "如果B+c发生中枢扩展，从5分钟扩展成30分钟的，那么a+A+b就是一个5分钟的走势类型" (第29课) [READ]. Leaving a 中枢 is late: its break is "当且仅当一个次级别走势离开该“缠中说禅走势中枢”后，其后的次级别回抽走势不重新回到该“缠中说禅走势中枢”内" (第18课), confirmed by the third-type point, "一个次级别走势类型向上离开缠中说禅走势中枢，然后以一个次级别走势类型回试，其低点不跌破ZG，则构成第三类买点" (第20课) [READ]: in the model a block that leaves is ±1 only once the next block is complete and has not returned, so the first bar of a ±1 piece is confirmed no earlier than the end of the block after it. Arriving is late too: the blocks that build the next 中枢 are ±1 until three of them overlap.

**The lag has no bound.** "一旦这中枢形成，该走势类型随时结束都是符合理论的" (第45课); a 盘整 "可以不结束，可以不断延伸下去，不断围绕这缠中说禅中枢上上下下地延伸下去直到无穷都是可以的" (第18课) [READ]. That every figure completes is asserted, "任何级别的任何走势类型终要完成" (第17课) [READ]; in the model it is a property of the market's law, assumed of none and unproved: a series whose every K-line has one range merges into one bar that no later K-line ends.

**At the merged bar the lag is one K-line at the least, and the bar's number and first K-line are known at once.** Whether K-line n merges into the bar before it or begins a new one is decided by the merged bar so far and K-line n (the order rule of 第65课), so which merged bar holds K-line n, and that bar's first K-line, are known at n; what is not known is its last K-line, until a K-line that does not merge arrives [INFER]. A 分型 names the middle of three adjacent merged bars (第62课), so it is known no earlier than the third, and final only when the three are [INFER].

**What the contract holds** [READ: `lean/Contract/Chanlun.lean`]. `MergedBar.confirmEnd : Option Nat`, "the K-line that confirmed its end: a position; not set while it is still merging"; `Line`, `Segment` and `Move`: "the merged bar at which it was confirmed: a bar; not set while it is still going on"; `Consolidation`: "not set while it is under construction". Each has the rule `endBeforeConfirmEnd`, for a merged bar `∀ c ∈ m.confirmEnd, m.«end» < c`. `Fractal` has no `confirmEnd`. No rule says that a record given with `confirmEnd` set is not given again changed.

**What the spec's requirements already force** [INFER from `covers`, `refers`, `records`, `endBeforeConfirmEnd`, and `Process.mono` (`lean/Kahn/Process.lean`) on logs, where `≤` is prefix (`lean/Kahn/Log.lean`)].
- Each `mergedBar` result that holds the K-line of the highest `index` read has `confirmEnd` not set: its `end` is that `index` (`covers`; `refers` allows no higher position), and a `confirmEnd` would be a position read and after it. On a clean series, where each K-line's `index` is its place (`cleaner`), that K-line is the last read; on an input whose indices do not ascend it need not be. The same holds of a `Line`, `Segment`, `Move` or `Consolidation` whose `end` is the highest `mIndex` among the results. So the unfinished last unit is in the requirements, whatever the library computes.
- Each K-line read of a clean series adds a `mergedBar` result whose `end` is its `index`: no earlier result may name it (`refers`), `covers` asks for one now, and what was given stays (`mono`). That two such results are one bar is in no requirement: `covers` asks only that the `index` is from `start` to `end`, and nothing fixes `mIndex`. Where a library merges K-lines, as 包含 does, its bar of several K-lines has a result for each K-line it took, and that they are versions of one bar is the library's to say.

**The options.** What the engine gives at each bar:
- Confirmed records alone, each final when given and never given again. What it costs: a record's latency is its confirmation lag; the unit going on is absent; and for merged bars `covers` rules it out.
- Confirmed records, and of each kind and level the one still going on, `confirmEnd` not set, given again as it grows and once more when confirmed. What it costs: versions on the topic, which a reader must tell apart (issue 3). For merged bars `covers` asks a result at each K-line, confirmed or not; that it is the same bar given again is the library's.
- Provisional records that may be withdrawn rather than refined (a 分型 the next bar removes, "分型可修改"; a higher-level record before the regrouping of 第29课 and 第32课 is settled). What it costs: neither the contract nor `Result` has a form of a withdrawal, so what is given stays in every reader's view.

**What it gives the spec.** `covers` asks no lag of the merged bars, and can: which merged bar holds a K-line is known at the K-line, only the bar's end is not [INFER]. For the records above, the spec asks nothing of when, and there is no bound to ask. What can be asked on the records alone, with no reference to the lessons: that a record given with `confirmEnd` set is final. It is the model's confirmation property and is in no rule and no requirement today; stating it needs the identity of a record across its versions (issue 3). A choice left: whether a record of a kind with no `confirmEnd`, `Fractal`, is given before it is final, since a reader cannot tell a provisional one from a final one.

**Gaps.** That a given library's `confirmEnd` is the model's moment of confirmation is proved in no source read. Whether the market's bars give every record a confirmation is a question for data.

## 3. Results that change on an append-only topic: how do stream systems publish a result later revised, and how does that relate to the definition?

**Kind:** engineering. **Bears on:** `publish` (Requirements); "when it gives again a record still going on are the worker's library's" (Features); "Which record still going on it draws over which is the page's" (Visualization); the second and third axioms of Order.

**Apache Flink.** "A continuous query never terminates and produces dynamic results - another dynamic table"; "Dynamic tables are foremost a logical concept"; a query either "only appends to the result table" or "updates previously emitted results", and "Queries that have to update previously emitted results need to maintain all emitted rows to update them" (Apache Flink documentation, Dynamic Tables, https://nightlies.apache.org/flink/flink-docs-release-2.3/docs/concepts/sql-table-concepts/dynamic_tables/, read whole) [READ]. A dynamic table's changes are encoded in a stream in three ways [READ, same page]:
- "Append-only stream: A dynamic table that is only modified by INSERT changes can be converted into a stream by emitting the inserted rows."
- "Retract stream": "encoding an INSERT change as add message, a DELETE change as a retract message, and an UPDATE change as a retract message for the updated (previous) row, and an additional message for the updating (new) row."
- "Upsert stream": it "requires a (possibly composite) unique key", "encoding INSERT and UPDATE changes as upsert messages and DELETE changes as delete messages. The stream consuming operator needs to be aware of the unique key attribute to apply messages correctly."

**Kafka Streams.** A KStream is a record stream, "where each data record represents a self-contained datum in the unbounded data set", its records "always interpreted as an "INSERT"", "because no record replaces an existing row with the same key"; a KTable is a changelog stream, "where each data record represents an update", "interpreted as an UPSERT aka INSERT/UPDATE because any existing row with the same key is overwritten", and "a record with a `null` value represents a "DELETE" or tombstone for the record's key" (Apache Kafka, Streams DSL guide, KStream, KTable, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/apache/kafka/trunk/docs/streams/developer-guide/dsl-api.md) [READ]. "A stream can be considered a changelog of a table", turned into the table "by replaying the changelog from beginning to end"; an aggregation's output is a KTable so that "the new value is considered to overwrite the old value with the same key in subsequent processing steps" (Apache Kafka, Streams Core Concepts, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/apache/kafka/trunk/docs/streams/core-concepts.md, read whole) [READ].

**What the two have in common.** A revision is a new record appended; the log is not edited. What makes the new record a revision of an old one is outside the log's order: a key (the upsert stream, the KTable), or the old row sent again as a retraction (the retract stream); the consumer applies it. With neither a key nor a retraction a stream is a record stream, each record standing alone [INFER from the two].

**Relation to the definition.**
- A revision is new output, never an edit: for `x ≤ y`, `fn x ≤ fn y` (`Process.mono`), and "What a process gives for a log is the limit of what it gives for the log's finite prefixes: no output waits for the end of the input" (`Process.eq_sup_take`) [READ: `lean/Kahn/Process.lean`, `lean/Kahn/Log.lean`]. That the topic keeps every version is a modelling choice: "A topic is seen as its log: everything ever appended to it", and "That a reader reads the whole log is assumed here, not proved"; the theorem gives its reason, "No view that replaces a record already given is a process of the log" (`Kafka.compaction_not_process`: no process gives `r₁` at position 0 for the log `r₁` and `r₂` at position 0 for the log `r₁ r₂`) [READ: `lean/Kafka.lean`]. So "the last version of each result" is a view a reader computes; it changes as the log grows, and no process of the definition gives it.
- The engine's topic has no key: `Kafka.Topic.ofName` sets `Key := Empty`. `Kafka.Record.value` is an `Option V`, and `publish` asks that each record's value is its result, so no record is a tombstone, and one would name nothing [READ: `lean/Kafka.lean`; spec, `publish`]. `Result` has no row kind and no case that withdraws. So as the definition stands, with no key and no identity stated, the topic is a record stream, each result standing alone. Were an identity stated, it would be an upsert stream whose key lies inside the value, which every reader must know, "aware of the unique key attribute to apply messages correctly" [INFER].
- The fields that could be that key: a merged bar's `mIndex`; a `Line`'s `start`; a `Segment`'s, a `Move`'s and a `Consolidation`'s `level` and `start`; a `Fractal`'s `mIndex`. The contract does not say they identify a record across its versions, nor that a record still going on keeps them: "which is still going on are their user's" [READ: `lean/Contract/Chanlun.lean`; the candidates INFER]. For a merged bar the lessons' order rule keeps them: its number and its first K-line do not change as it merges [INFER from 第65课]. For the others it is the library's to hold.
- The encodings against the definition [INFER]. Append-only fits a topic of confirmed results alone. Upsert fits versions under a stated identity: each version is the whole record, the reader overwriting by identity, which is what the page does when it draws one record over another. Retract needs a retraction as a result, which `Result` has no form of, as a correction has no form of a deletion (spec, The data); it is what withdrawing a provisional record would take.
- Order. A version is later than the one it revises by its position in the one partition, written in the worker's order (`publish`) and kept (Order, third axiom): "later" needs no clock and no event time [INFER].

**The options.**
- Versions by a stated identity: the spec names the fields that identify a record of each kind; each version is the whole record; a record with `confirmEnd` set is its last. What it costs: it binds the library to keep those fields while a record goes on, and to give no provisional record it may have to withdraw; the log holds every version, growing with them.
- Versions by the library's convention (what the Features sentence leaves today). What it costs: each reader depends on a rule no contract states; `chart` cannot say which records a chart shows as current; "a confirmed record is final" cannot be stated.
- A form that withdraws a version, as a seventh case of `Result` or as a key with a null value. What it costs: a message that is no record of the contract, or a key on a topic the spec gives none; and a rule pairing a withdrawal with what it withdraws.
- Confirmed results only above the merged bar (issue 2): no versions there. What it costs: the latency; the merged bars still have a result for each K-line, by `covers`.

**What it gives the spec.** `publish` is the right requirement for a topic that carries revisions: the revision is in the order, and the three axioms of Order keep it. `covers` puts a `mergedBar` result on the topic for each K-line, several for a bar that merges, so the spec cannot be silent on how they are read without leaving the result series ambiguous: the identity is either stated or left, by name, to the library. If stated, it is a definition for each kind, and one requirement on the results of every input follows it: that no result with the same identity and other fields comes after one with `confirmEnd` set. That a reader's current record of an identity is its last version is a rule for readers, which the spec gives to the page. Whether a withdrawal has a form is a choice; without one, what a library gives provisionally can only be refined.

**Gaps.** The Streams DSL guide was read at its sections on KStream, KTable and GlobalKTable and at every line naming a tombstone, not whole. No source read treats an upsert stream whose key is inside the value on a topic with no key; the reading above is inferred from Flink's unique-key condition.

## 4. Incremental and batch agreeing: results computed bar by bar the same as on the whole series

**Kind:** modelling. **Bears on:** "Every part is a Kahn process: more input never takes back output, and no output waits for the end of the input" (What it guarantees); `worker` and `publish`; "How the worker computes its results, in what order it gives them … are the worker's library's" (Features); "each reader reads it from its start" (Order).

**In the definition the two are one function.** `worker : Process (Kafka.Partition Market.KLine) (Log MarketModelEngine.Result)` [READ: `lean/MarketModelEngine.lean`]: its results are a function of the log read; the results on the whole series are the limit of the results on its prefixes (`Process.eq_sup_take`, `lean/Kahn/Log.lean`), and the results after n K-lines are a prefix of the results after n + 1 (`Process.mono`, `lean/Kahn/Process.lean`, `≤` on logs being prefix) [READ]. So the worker's output on a finite series is what it has given bar by bar, every version included: how many K-lines it reads at a time cannot change its log, and a library whose provisional records depend on that is no one function of its input [INFER].

**A batch function is not the worker; it is a view of the worker's log.** A function that returns the records of a finished series returns, for a longer series, other records in place of some: it is not monotone (`Process.mono`), so it is no worker [INFER]. Its relation to the worker is that of a table to its changelog, as Flink says of its two modes: "Once the changelog is applied to an external system (e.g. a key-value store), one can see that both modes are able to produce exactly the same output table. By consuming all input data before emitting results, the changelog of the batch mode consists solely of insert-only changes" (Apache Flink documentation, DataStream API Integration, https://nightlies.apache.org/flink/flink-docs-stable/docs/dev/table/data_stream_api/, the changelog sections) [READ]. What agrees between the two modes is the table; the changelogs differ. For the engine: the view of the bar-by-bar log, the last version of each identity, equals the batch result on the same series, which can be stated only once the identity is (issue 3) [INFER].

**The results are a function of the series and of the rule's parameters.** The lessons leave these to whoever computes [READ for each sentence; that they are free choices, INFER]:
- The first merge's direction: a merged group goes up or down by the bar before it ("第n根与第n-1根不是包含关系，那么如果gn>=gn-1，那么称第n-1、n、n+1根K线是向上的；如果dn<=dn-1，那么称第n-1、n、n+1根K线是向下的", 第65课), which the series' first K-lines do not have.
- Whether the block that leaves a 中枢 may itself open the next: "在趋势中，连接两个同级别“缠中说禅走势中枢”的必然是次级别以下级别的走势类型" (第18课).
- A limit on a 中枢's extension: "一般来说，中枢的延伸不能超过5段，也就是一旦出现6段的延伸，加上形成中枢本身那三段，就构成更大级别的中枢了" (第33课) and "m30<9，否则就会变成周的中枢了" (第36课), against "无论这个延伸有多长时间，都不可能产生更大级别的走势中枢" (第20课).
- What a return is: the block's range meets the 中枢 ("走势中枢的延伸等价于任意区间[dn，gn]与[ZD，ZG]有重叠", 第20课), or its end lies inside.
